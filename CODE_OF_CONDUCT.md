# Code of conduct

Participants must be respectful, specific and constructive. Harassment, personal attacks,
discrimination, deliberate disruption and publication of another person's private information are
not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict
participation when necessary. Enforcement decisions should be based on documented behavior, not on
technical disagreement or criticism made in good faith.

Report conduct concerns to `qubitcoin@superquantum.com`. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as practical.
